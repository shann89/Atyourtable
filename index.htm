Part-Time Cook

Margaree Meals on Wheels

Job Summary

The Cook is the heart of our program, responsible for preparing delicious, nutritious, and safe meals for 40 community members. This role involves planning and executing a three-course menu (soup, main, dessert) three days a week, ensuring all food is prepared to the highest standard and packaged for delivery. The ideal candidate is passionate about food, reliable, and dedicated to making a positive impact on the lives of others.

Key Responsibilities

  • Meal Planning & Preparation: Plan rotating, well-balanced three-course meals considering dietary needs. Cook high-quality meals for 40 participants, 3x per week. Manage inventory and minimize waste.
  • Food Safety & Kitchen Management: Maintain excellent hygiene, adhering to all NS food safety regulations. Keep kitchen/equipment clean and organized. Properly label/store food. Oversee safe packaging.
  • Team Collaboration: Work closely with the Program Coordinator and volunteers. Provide clear direction to kitchen assistants. Assist with meal payment management and food purchasing.

Schedule & Hours

  • Part-time position: Approximately 15 hours per week.
  • Workdays: Three days per week (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri).
  • Cooking completed for midday meal delivery (ready by 11:30 am).

Qualifications & Skills

  • 2+ years professional cooking experience (kitchens, catering, or institutional).
  • Valid Food Handler Certification is required.
  • Strong knowledge of cooking techniques, nutrition, and sanitation.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Reliable, organized, and detail-oriented with some computer skills.
  • Asset: Experience cooking for seniors or managing special dietary needs.

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to stand for extended periods (3-4 hours).
  • Lift and carry up to 25 lbs.
